## Featured project

### [Predicting medical aid uptake among young Namibians](https://github.com/Rauna-creator/MSc-Capstone-Project)
My M.Sc. capstone, built for a real client, Namibia Medical Care.
- Surveyed **553 young people (18–35)** and trained Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers. **XGBoost** performed best (**92% accuracy, macro F1 0.92**).
- Used sentiment analysis (TextBlob) and LDA topic modelling (Gensim) on open-text answers.
- **Key insight:** most respondents would pay under N$500/month, below the N$735 entry price.
- The findings shaped a campaign that generated **~2,000 leads and 400+ sign-ups**.
- [Live dashboard](https://trdt2hfg7vsrgfmykynypf.streamlit.app/)
